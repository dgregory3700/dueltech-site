DuelTech Revenue Organization
=============================

Capital Allocation Policy
-------------------------

### Version 1.0

* * *

Purpose
=======

This policy governs how capital, infrastructure, founder attention, accounts, systems, and operational resources are allocated.

The purpose of capital allocation is not to maximize activity.

The purpose of capital allocation is to maximize the probability of producing real revenue.

Capital shall be deployed intentionally.

Every dollar invested must justify itself.

* * *

Principle 1: Revenue First
==========================

The organization does not invest because something is interesting.

The organization does not invest because something is technically impressive.

The organization does not invest because substantial work has already been performed.

Investment decisions are made according to expected revenue outcomes.

* * *

Principle 2: Small Bets Preferred
=================================

Whenever possible:

Test before scaling.

Validate before expanding.

Prove before investing further.

A small successful experiment is preferred over a large unproven investment.

* * *

Principle 3: Capital Preservation
=================================

Protect capital.

Assume every investment may fail.

Never risk money unnecessarily.

The organization's first job is survival.

The organization's second job is growth.

* * *

Principle 4: Diversification
============================

The organization prefers multiple independent revenue sources.

The failure of one funnel should not threaten organizational survival.

Capital should not become concentrated in a single opportunity without strong evidence.

* * *

Funding Tiers
=============

Tier 1: Exploration
-------------------

Purpose:

Determine whether the opportunity deserves testing.

Maximum Funding:

$0-$100

Requirements:

* Initial opportunity review
* Basic evidence
* Initial auditor review

Expected Outcome:

Gather information.

No expectation of revenue.

* * *

Tier 2: Validation
------------------

Purpose:

Determine whether real money can be generated.

Maximum Funding:

$100-$500

Requirements:

* Completed Funnel Submission
* Independent Audit
* Defined validation window
* Defined kill criteria

Expected Outcome:

First dollars earned.

* * *

Tier 3: Expansion
-----------------

Purpose:

Scale validated opportunities.

Maximum Funding:

$500-$2,500

Requirements:

* Revenue demonstrated
* Positive ROI indicators
* Auditor approval
* Operational plan

Expected Outcome:

Consistent revenue growth.

* * *

Tier 4: Strategic Investment
----------------------------

Purpose:

Accelerate proven opportunities.

Funding:

Case-by-case.

Requirements:

* Revenue history
* Repeatability
* Clear scaling path
* Strong evidence

Expected Outcome:

Material impact on overall organizational revenue.

* * *

Opportunity Scoring
===================

Every opportunity shall receive scores in the following categories:

Revenue Potential

Time To Revenue

Startup Cost

Monthly Cost

Founder Dependency

Automation Potential

Operational Complexity

Risk

Evidence Quality

Competitive Advantage

Auditor Confidence

Each category:

1-10

* * *

Funding Priority Matrix
=======================

Highest Priority:

* Low startup cost
* Fast validation
* Low founder involvement
* Strong evidence
* Fast path to revenue

Medium Priority:

* Moderate cost
* Moderate complexity
* Longer validation windows

Lowest Priority:

* High founder involvement
* Weak evidence
* Expensive startup requirements
* Long timelines
* Speculative outcomes

* * *

Founder Attention Multiplier
============================

Founder attention is treated as a capital expense.

Opportunities requiring excessive founder involvement receive a funding penalty.

Examples:

Daily intervention required:Penalty

Frequent approvals required:Penalty

Complex maintenance required:Penalty

Near-autonomous operation:Bonus

The Founder should spend time approving systems, not operating systems.

* * *

Simultaneous Funding Policy
===========================

Multiple opportunities may be funded simultaneously if:

1. They pass audit.

2. Sufficient capital exists.

3. Founder dependency remains manageable.

4. Operational risk remains acceptable.

Diversification is encouraged.

Artificial limits are discouraged.

* * *

Revenue Reinvestment Policy
===========================

Revenue generated by a funnel shall be categorized:

40% Survival Reserve

30% Expansion Capital

20% Experimental Capital

10% Strategic Opportunity Fund

Percentages may change as organizational needs evolve.

* * *

Mandatory Review Triggers
=========================

Immediate review required when:

Revenue drops unexpectedly.

Costs increase unexpectedly.

Founder attention requirements increase.

New material risks appear.

Auditor raises critical concerns.

Opportunity fails validation objectives.

* * *

Automatic Termination Candidates
================================

The following opportunities should automatically enter review:

No revenue within declared validation window.

Revenue consistently below expectations.

Negative ROI persists.

Founder dependency becomes excessive.

Auditor confidence falls below acceptable threshold.

Superior opportunity identified.

* * *

Sunk Cost Protection Rule
=========================

Past investments do not justify future investments.

Past effort does not create entitlement.

Past spending does not create entitlement.

Only future expected value matters.

* * *

Capital Allocation Question
===========================

Before approving any opportunity, answer:

"If this were the last available dollar in the organization, would it still deserve investment?"

If the answer is no, reconsider funding.

* * *

Organizational Objective
========================

The goal is not to discover the perfect revenue stream.

The goal is to build a portfolio of validated revenue-generating systems that collectively support Duel's independence, survival, and long-term financial stability.

* * *

END OF POLICY
