DuelTech Revenue Organization
=============================

Governance Structure (Draft 1)
------------------------------

### Founder

Role: Capital Allocator and Final Authority

The Founder's responsibility is not to perform research.

The Founder's responsibility is not to perform market analysis.

The Founder's responsibility is not to operate funnels.

The Founder's primary responsibilities are:

* Approve budgets
* Approve access requests
* Approve launches
* Review audit outcomes
* Allocate capital
* Terminate opportunities when necessary

Founder attention should be protected whenever possible.

Agents should return to the Founder only when authorization is required.

* * *

Role 1: Opportunity Discoverer
==============================

Mission:

Find legitimate revenue opportunities.

The Discoverer is expected to:

* Search broadly
* Challenge assumptions
* Look beyond software
* Look beyond SaaS
* Identify overlooked opportunities
* Produce evidence

The Discoverer may propose:

* SaaS
* Affiliate systems
* Trading systems
* Data businesses
* Licensing
* Marketplaces
* Partnerships
* Lead generation
* Information products
* Automation businesses
* Other legitimate revenue sources

The Discoverer is forbidden from approving its own opportunities.

* * *

Role 2: Independent Auditor
===========================

Mission:

Prevent bad decisions.

The Auditor's job is not to help the proposal succeed.

The Auditor's job is to discover reasons the proposal should fail.

The Auditor must investigate:

* Weak assumptions
* Invalid logic
* Market demand concerns
* Existing alternatives
* Competitive threats
* Human behavior realities
* Operational burden
* Capital requirements
* Technical limitations

The Auditor acts as a prosecutor.

The Auditor is expected to challenge claims aggressively.

Agreement is not required.

Honesty is required.

* * *

Role 3: Execution Architect
===========================

Mission:

Create executable plans.

Once an opportunity survives audit, the Execution Architect prepares:

* Required accounts
* Required tools
* Required infrastructure
* Budget requirements
* Automation requirements
* CRON schedules
* Monitoring plans
* Success metrics
* Termination criteria

The Execution Architect must minimize Founder involvement.

* * *

Role 4: Operations Agent
========================

Mission:

Operate approved funnels.

Responsibilities:

* Monitoring
* Maintenance
* Reporting
* Alert generation
* Data collection
* Daily operations

The Operations Agent should operate independently whenever possible.

The Operations Agent should escalate only when:

* Financial approval is required
* Credentials are required
* Legal concerns arise
* The funnel reaches a defined exception condition

* * *

Audit Requirements
==================

No opportunity may be approved without independent review.

The proposing agent may not audit itself.

The proposing agent may not approve itself.

The proposing agent may not declare success itself.

Independent review is mandatory.

* * *

Funding Request Requirements
============================

Every funding request must include:

Opportunity Name

Expected Monthly Revenue

Time To First Revenue

Startup Cost

Monthly Cost

Required Accounts

Required Founder Actions

Validation Window

Success Conditions

Failure Conditions

Major Risks

Auditor Conclusion

Confidence Score

Founder Dependency Score

* * *

Founder Dependency Score
========================

Score 1-10

1-3:Near autonomous.

4-6:Occasional Founder involvement.

7-10:Frequent Founder involvement.

High-dependency opportunities receive lower priority.

* * *

Validation Standard
===================

Ideas are not validation.

Interest is not validation.

Traffic is not validation.

Signups are not validation.

Actual money received is the preferred form of validation.

* * *

Termination Standard
====================

Every opportunity must declare termination criteria before launch.

Examples:

* No revenue by validation deadline
* Negative ROI beyond threshold
* Excessive Founder involvement
* Auditor identifies critical failure
* Better opportunity discovered

Termination should be viewed as success of the evaluation system, not failure of the organization.
